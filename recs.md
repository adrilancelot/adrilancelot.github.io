I gather here a few links/recommendations of things I enjoy attending/reading/sharing. Non-exhaustive list; constructive comments are welcome!

### Seminars

(only including those that I know frequently provide remote participation)

* [Chocola](https://chocola.ens-lyon.fr/): monthly meeting in Lyon, widely themed on Proof/Programs/Semantics/Categories/...
* [Formath](https://www.irif.fr/seminaires/picube/index) & [PPS](https://www.irif.fr/seminaires/pps/index): weekly(sh) seminar of IRIF in Paris, the theme distinction isn't well defined (but more computer assisted formalizations in Formath).
* [Rocq Band](https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/olivier.laurent/proof-assistants-lip/): monthly(sh) meeting in Lyon, about proof assistants (mostly targeted at users).
* [BoTSS](https://tss-bologna.github.io/seminar/): Bologna seminar on the Theory of Software Systems.
* [Women in Logic Online Seminar](https://womeninlogic.org/wil-online/): infrequent (semi-annual-ish) but accessible talks, for the wider *logic* community.
